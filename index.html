📖 HOW TO PLAY & SECRETS

Pedestrian Safety Guide

Learn the crosswalk rules, how signals work, and the secret Cybertruck!

🎮 Simple Game Mechanics

Move in All Directions (Mouse & Keys)

Click & hold anywhere on screen to have your explorer smoothly follow the mouse in all directions! You can also use [W/A/S/D], [Arrow Keys], or the on-screen buttons.

🟡
Stay on the Sidewalk & Wait at Curbs

Always walk along the grey sidewalk path. When you reach an intersection, STOP behind the yellow tactile pavers and check the corner signal!

🛎️
Press the Crosswalk Button [SPACEBAR]

At traffic intersections, walk up to the yellow pedestrian signal button on the post and press [SPACEBAR] (or tap the sign) to request the crosswalk! Then wait safely on the curb until it turns green to WALK!

💥
Don't Cross at the Wrong Time!

If you rush into the road while the signal says STOP or FLASHING, your character will stumble and sit on the ground to recover!

🚦 How the Crosswalk Signs Work

🛑 SOLID STOP HAND

WAIT ON SIDEWALK: Cars have the green light. Stand safely on the curb and wait patiently for your turn.

⏳ FLASHING WALK SIGN

DO NOT START CROSSING: The walk time is ending! You must wait at the curb for the complete cycle:
1. Wait on Flashing ⏳ ➡️ 2. Wait on STOP Hand 🛑 ➡️ 3. Cross on Green WALK 🟢

🟢 SOLID WALK SIGN

SAFE TO CROSS: Cars halt at the stop line! Walk steadily across the white zebra stripes to the opposite sidewalk.

⚡ Secret Vehicle: The Cybertruck

🏆 FLAWLESS RUN SECRET

How to See the Cybertruck:

  • Rule 1: Complete your entire walk from Home to Grandma's Bakery WITHOUT FALLING DOWN AT ALL!
  • Rule 2: Wait patiently at every curb and only cross when the signal is solid green.
  • Rule 3: The moment you step onto the opposite sidewalk after completing your final street cross, the Cybertruck will drive by across the road behind you!
  • Note: It only drives by ONCE, so keep your eyes open!
